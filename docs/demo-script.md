# 3–5 Minute Demonstration Walkthrough Script

1. **Opening Comparison (`/`)**:
   - Introduce the core problem: classroom Mercator maps show high-latitude countries massively enlarged.
   - Slide the reveal slider: watch Greenland shrink to true scale and Africa assert its true continental dominance (~14.0× larger).
2. **Distortion Explorer (`/explore`)**:
   - Inspect the world choropleth. Hover over Greenland (+345% enlargement), Canada (+275%), Norway (+362%), and Dem. Rep. Congo (+2%).
   - Show the sortable leaderboard and export CSV.
3. **Latitude Math (`/learn`)**:
   - Drag the slider from -80° to +80°. Show the mathematical reason: $J(\phi) = \sec^2(\phi)$.
   - Inspect the identical 500km disks expanding with latitude.
4. **Move Lab (`/lab`)**:
   - Pick Greenland and drag it to the equator (over Central Africa). See it fit inside DR Congo.
   - Verify area invariance indicator (100.0% preserved).
5. **Country vs. Country (`/compare`)**:
   - Select *Greenland vs. DR Congo*. Show side-by-side silhouettes at identical scale.
6. **Visual Power Gap (`/representation`)**:
   - Show diverging bar chart of map share % vs. land share %.
7. **"World According To..." (`/world`)**:
   - Switch from Land Area to Population (watch India and China swell).
   - Switch to GDP (watch North America and Europe expand).
